## Format: `[ID] [P?] [HITL|OD:D#|POLICY?] [Story] Description`

- **[P]**: parallel-safe (disjoint files, no incomplete dependency)
- **[HITL]**: governor step (account, vault, approval, rating, or review work agents cannot do); implement stops and asks in plain language
- **[OD:D#]**: resolves Open Decision D# (D3, D8, D9, D10); nothing that depends on it starts before it
- **[POLICY]**: process adjudication the orchestrator may do under written policy
- **Lanes** (spec § Delivery lanes, plan § Phased delivery): Models+tests, Durability, Ship, Evals. One lane = one worker at a time with disjoint owned paths (§ Owned paths). At most 3 workers run at once across the lab.
